Inbox Cleaner

Inbox Cleaner is a personal, self-hosted tool that helps its owner tidy their own Gmail mailbox. It runs on the owner's own computers and is not offered to the public.

What it does

  • Reads the owner's Gmail messages and groups them by sender.
  • Sorts mail into categories so the owner can decide what to keep.
  • Applies the owner's decisions in Gmail: labelling, archiving and moving mail to the trash.
  • Sends unsubscribe requests to mailing lists the owner chooses to leave.

Who can use it

Only its owner, signed in to their own Google account. There are no other users and no sign-up.
